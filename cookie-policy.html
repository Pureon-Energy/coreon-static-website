Cookie & Privacy Policy

Draft — last updated 2026-09-16

This page explains what this website stores on your device and why. It covers this static marketing site only — not any Coreon product, dashboard, or customer account.

Note for Coreon: this is a technically accurate draft of what the site does today, written to be reviewed and signed off before publishing — it is not a substitute for legal review. If analytics, ads, or other third-party scripts are added later, this page (and the consent banner's behaviour) needs to be updated to match before those scripts run.

What this site stores, and why

NameTypePurposeDuration
coreon-lang localStorage Remembers whether you last viewed the site in English or Danish. Until you clear your browser's site data.
coreon-cookie-consent localStorage Remembers that you've seen this cookie notice, and whether you accepted or declined it, so it isn't shown again on every visit. Until you clear your browser's site data.

Neither of these is a tracking cookie: they aren't sent to any server, aren't used to identify you across sites, and aren't shared with anyone. They only ever live in your own browser.

Third-party requests

This site loads its typefaces (Poppins, Work Sans, IBM Plex Mono) from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). Loading a font involves a request to Google's servers, which — like any web request — includes your IP address. Google's handling of that request is covered by Google's own privacy policy. No Coreon-specific tracking is layered on top of this.

The contact form

The "Get in touch" form does not submit to a server. Pressing send opens your own email application with the message pre-filled, addressed to hello@coreon.dk. Nothing you type is transmitted until you choose to send that email yourself.

Analytics & advertising

At the time of writing, this site runs no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts of any kind.

Your choices

  • The cookie banner lets you accept or decline the essential local storage described above.
  • You can clear coreon-lang and coreon-cookie-consent at any time via your browser's site data / privacy settings — the site will simply ask again and default back to English.
  • Blocking localStorage entirely won't break the site; it will just forget your language choice and re-show the cookie notice on your next visit.
