FAQ

Questions worth asking before you say yes.

Do we need to install new equipment?

Just one smart optimization device — no fleet of new machinery, no major installation project. It connects to what you already have and does the work in the background.

Will this disrupt how we operate today?

No. You keep running your business exactly as before — we optimize in the background once you've approved the plan.

How much could we realistically save?

Typical savings are 10–30% on energy costs, with most customers seeing a return in under 6 months. Actual savings depend on your setup — the first conversation is where we map what's realistic for you.

What does the first step actually involve?

A short, honest conversation about how your operations run today — about 15 minutes. No forms, no long onboarding.

What happens to our data?

It's used only to power your optimization — never sold or shared with third parties. If you have specific data-handling or security requirements, we'll go through them before you commit to anything.
